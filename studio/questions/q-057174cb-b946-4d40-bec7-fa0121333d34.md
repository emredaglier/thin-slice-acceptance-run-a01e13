Allowed. `next@16.4.5` is already in the house stack: `apps/web` and `apps/cms` pin that exact version, so nothing new comes into the lockfile. Adding it as a devDependency of `packages/ui` lets `fonts.ts` typecheck its `next/font/google` import. Keep the version pinned to 16.4.5, the same as the apps. Commit the lockfile change along with it, and run `pnpm lint`, `typecheck`, `test` and `check:assets` before you hand off.
