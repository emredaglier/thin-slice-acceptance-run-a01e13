Plan check for Kiln & Clay: G4 scope and what the owner judges at G4 (2026-11-20).

## Notes

- At G4 the studio preview shows the one page in this order:
  - the hero, with the studio name and pitch;
  - About;
  - Classes: three classes, each with a name, a length and a price (for example €45), plus "Prices include VAT.";
  - Contact: a working email link and the full street address;
  - the footer, with the legal block and the privacy note.
- The final look is in place by G4: off-white background, clay-brown accent, Fraunces headings, system sans body text.
- The G4 document will give:
  - the results of the four checks (lint, typecheck, test, check:assets);
  - the contrast ratios, computed and not measured in a browser;
  - the drafted pitch and About text, for the owner to approve or change.
- No agent can see the page. The owner checks the layout on a phone at 360 px.
- The one risk to G4 is that build has no slack:
  - the two slack days sit before G5, not before G4;
  - ticket 01 also carries the starter setup (the workspace rename and the placeholder text);
  - ticket 02 starts only when ticket 01 merges, so a lost day in setup or a failing check moves G4 by that day.

## Decisions

- G4 scope stays as planned: tickets 01 and 02, with no new tickets.
- At G4 the owner judges three things: the page on the preview, the pitch and About wording, and the 360 px phone layout.
- If build runs late, the producer says so in the G4 document and proposes cuts under `## Scope cuts`. Checks are never skipped to make the date.
- The schedule does not change: G4 on 2026-11-20, G5 on 2026-11-27, launch on 2026-12-01.
